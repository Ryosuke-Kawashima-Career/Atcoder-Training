THE INVARIANT
Keep turning left.
Each stack keeps a convex chain. A right turn bends inward; a straight turn leaves an unnecessary middle point. Pop that middle point and test again before pushing the candidate.

      
WHY IT’S FAST
Every point enters and leaves once.
Sorting costs O(n log n). After that, both scans are O(n): although a step can pop several points, each point is pushed once and popped at most once per chain.
